Loyalty Club Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: September 2026

By joining and using The Bunny Loyalty Club, you agree to the following Terms & Conditions. Please read these terms carefully before participating in the loyalty program.

1. About the Loyalty Program

The Bunny Loyalty Club is a customer loyalty and rewards program operated by:

The Bunny Luxury Hair & Beauty Studio

The program allows eligible customers to collect loyalty stamps for qualifying salon visits and unlock a complimentary eligible salon service/package after completing the required number of qualifying visits.

The program is intended to reward genuine and regular customers of the salon.

2. How the Loyalty Program Works

Eligible customers can collect up to 10 loyalty stamps in one reward cycle.

A customer becomes eligible for the reward after successfully completing 10 qualifying visits.

After the 10th qualifying visit is approved, the customer will receive one loyalty reward with a stated value of up to ₹3,000, subject to these Terms & Conditions and the salon's eligible reward menu.

After the reward is successfully redeemed, the customer's loyalty cycle will restart from 0.

Previous visits and redeemed rewards may remain recorded in the salon's loyalty system for record-keeping, fraud prevention and program administration.

3. Qualifying Visit

A loyalty stamp is issued only for a genuine, completed and paid salon visit.

To qualify for one stamp:

  • The customer's final eligible bill must meet the salon's minimum qualifying spend.
  • The current minimum qualifying spend is ₹600 per visit, unless otherwise displayed by the salon.
  • The transaction must be genuine and actually paid for.
  • The customer must receive an eligible salon service.
  • The visit must be verified through the salon's loyalty system.
  • Only one stamp may be issued to one customer for one qualifying visit.

The salon may change the minimum qualifying spend for future visits or future loyalty cycles by updating the program terms.

4. One Stamp Per Visit

Only one loyalty stamp can be earned from one qualifying visit, regardless of the amount spent.

For example:

  • A customer spends ₹600 → 1 stamp.
  • A customer spends ₹2,000 → 1 stamp.
  • A customer spends ₹5,000 → 1 stamp.

Higher spending does not automatically provide multiple stamps unless the salon specifically introduces a different promotion.

5. Same-Day Transactions

A customer cannot obtain multiple loyalty stamps by splitting transactions or making multiple purchases on the same day.

Multiple bills created for the purpose of obtaining additional stamps may be treated as one qualifying visit.

The salon may combine related transactions made on the same day when determining loyalty eligibility.

6. Genuine Visits Only

Loyalty stamps are intended only for genuine visits to the salon.

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Fake visits
  • Fake transactions
  • Repeated QR scanning without a genuine salon visit
  • Manipulating the website
  • Manipulating browser storage
  • Attempting to modify loyalty data
  • Attempting to create duplicate accounts
  • Using another customer's loyalty account
  • Sharing or transferring loyalty access
  • Attempting to obtain stamps without receiving eligible services
  • Exploiting technical errors or system bugs
  • Using automated scripts, bots or other technical methods to generate stamps
  • Attempting to bypass program restrictions
  • Fraudulent or reversed payments
  • Chargeback transactions used to obtain loyalty benefits
  • Any other activity intended to obtain an unfair loyalty benefit

7. QR Code Usage

The salon's QR code is provided only as an access method to the loyalty program.

Scanning the QR code does not automatically guarantee a loyalty stamp.

A visit may require confirmation or approval by the salon team.

Taking a photograph of the QR code, scanning it outside the salon or repeatedly accessing the website does not constitute a qualifying salon visit.

8. Loyalty Account

A loyalty account is associated with the customer's registered information and/or authenticated loyalty identity.

Customers must provide accurate information during registration.

Customers must not:

  • Register using another person's information.
  • Create multiple accounts to obtain additional rewards.
  • Provide false information.
  • Attempt to access another customer's loyalty account.

The salon may suspend or cancel accounts where fraudulent, abusive or suspicious activity is detected.

9. Mobile Number and Customer Information

The mobile number provided during registration may be used to:

  • Identify the loyalty account.
  • Maintain loyalty records.
  • Assist with account recovery.
  • Verify eligibility.
  • Contact the customer regarding loyalty benefits where appropriate.

The salon will use customer information for legitimate business and loyalty-program purposes and will take reasonable measures to protect it.

Customers should not share access to their loyalty account or device with another person.

10. Loyalty Stamp Verification

A stamp is considered valid only when it is successfully recorded in the salon's official loyalty system.

Screenshots, manually altered images, copied QR codes, edited browser information or other unofficial representations do not constitute proof of a valid stamp.

The salon's digital loyalty records will be treated as the primary record for determining eligibility.

11. ₹3,000 Reward

After completing 10 valid qualifying visits, the customer may unlock a complimentary salon service/package with a stated retail/service value of up to ₹3,000.

The reward is:

  • A service benefit only.
  • Not cash.
  • Not a cash-equivalent balance.
  • Not redeemable for money.
  • Not refundable.
  • Not transferable.
  • Not convertible into another customer's loyalty account.

The ₹3,000 value represents the maximum eligible service/package value and does not mean that ₹3,000 cash or ₹3,000 account credit will be provided.

12. Eligible Reward Services

The ₹3,000 reward may be redeemed only against services/packages specifically designated by The Bunny Luxury Hair & Beauty Studio as eligible loyalty rewards.

The salon may exclude:

  • Certain premium services.
  • Retail products.
  • Hair/beauty products.
  • Gift cards.
  • Memberships.
  • Packages not included in the loyalty reward menu.
  • Third-party services.
  • Special event/promotional services.
  • Services with unusually high material or third-party costs.

The salon will communicate the eligible reward options at the time of redemption.

13. Reward Does Not Guarantee Any Particular Service

The ₹3,000 reward does not guarantee availability of a particular service, employee, date or appointment time.

Appointments may be required.

Normal salon appointment availability applies.

14. Reward Expiry

Each unlocked reward is valid for 30 days from the date it is issued, unless another validity period is clearly displayed by the salon.

Expired rewards cannot normally be reinstated or exchanged for cash.

The salon may, at its discretion, extend an expired reward in exceptional circumstances.

15. No Cash Alternative

The loyalty reward cannot be:

  • Converted to cash.
  • Transferred to a bank account.
  • Used as cash balance.
  • Exchanged for a refund.
  • Exchanged for another monetary benefit.

If the selected eligible service has a value below ₹3,000, the unused difference is not payable in cash or transferable to another service.

16. No Combining Offers

The loyalty reward generally cannot be combined with:

  • Discount coupons.
  • Promotional offers.
  • Other loyalty rewards.
  • Referral rewards.
  • Membership discounts.
  • Special event discounts.

Unless expressly permitted by The Bunny Luxury Hair & Beauty Studio.

17. Additional Charges

If a customer chooses a service, upgrade, add-on or package whose applicable price exceeds the value covered by the loyalty reward, the customer may be required to pay the applicable difference.

The salon will communicate any additional amount before providing the upgraded service.

18. Reward Redemption

To redeem a reward, the customer must present the active loyalty reward to the salon team.

The salon may verify:

  • Customer identity.
  • Loyalty account.
  • Reward status.
  • Reward validity.
  • Reward history.

The reward must show as Available in the salon's official loyalty system.

A reward already marked as redeemed cannot be reused.

19. Fraud and Abuse

The Bunny Luxury Hair & Beauty Studio reserves the right to investigate suspected misuse of the loyalty program.

If the salon reasonably believes that a customer has:

  • Manipulated the loyalty system.
  • Obtained stamps fraudulently.
  • Created duplicate accounts.
  • Used another customer's account.
  • Attempted to exploit a technical vulnerability.
  • Used false transactions.
  • Violated these Terms & Conditions.

the salon may, subject to applicable law:

  • Reject a suspicious stamp.
  • Reject a suspicious visit.
  • Cancel fraudulent stamps.
  • Suspend the loyalty account.
  • Cancel an improperly obtained reward.
  • Refuse redemption of an improperly obtained reward.
  • Restrict future participation in the program.

20. Technical Errors

If a technical error incorrectly adds stamps, creates rewards or displays incorrect loyalty information, the salon reserves the right to correct the error after verification.

A technical error does not automatically create an entitlement to an unintended reward.

The salon may correct inaccurate loyalty records to reflect genuine qualifying transactions.

21. No Guaranteed Reward for Invalid Transactions

Cancelled, refunded, reversed, disputed or fraudulent transactions may not qualify for loyalty stamps.

If a transaction that generated a loyalty stamp is subsequently refunded, cancelled or reversed, the corresponding loyalty benefit may be removed or adjusted.

22. Account Sharing

Loyalty accounts are intended for individual customers.

Customers should not share their loyalty account, authentication access, QR/session information or loyalty credentials with another person.

The salon may refuse to combine loyalty histories belonging to different people.

23. Lost Device or Browser

If a customer loses access to their device or browser, they should contact the salon team for assistance.

The salon may request reasonable information to verify ownership before restoring access to a loyalty account.

The salon is not required to transfer loyalty benefits where ownership cannot reasonably be verified.

24. Changes to the Program

The Bunny Luxury Hair & Beauty Studio may modify:

  • Minimum qualifying spend.
  • Eligible services.
  • Reward conditions.
  • Reward validity.
  • Program rules.
  • Program design.
  • Technical implementation.

Changes will generally apply to future visits/reward cycles and will not intentionally remove an already-valid redeemed or available reward except where required because of fraud, misuse, technical error or applicable law.

25. Program Suspension or Termination

The salon may temporarily suspend or permanently discontinue the loyalty program.

Where reasonably practical, customers will be informed of significant changes.

Rewards already valid at the time of program suspension may be handled according to the circumstances and applicable law.

26. Salon Records

The salon's official digital loyalty records will be used to determine:

  • Number of valid visits.
  • Stamp count.
  • Reward eligibility.
  • Reward issuance.
  • Reward redemption.

Screenshots or customer claims that conflict with the official system may require verification.

27. Fair Use

The loyalty program is designed for genuine customers who regularly use salon services.

The salon may apply reasonable fair-use controls to prevent abuse while maintaining the intended customer benefit.

28. No Transfer of Reward

A loyalty reward cannot be sold, transferred, exchanged or assigned to another person unless the salon expressly permits such transfer.

29. Acceptance

By registering for or using The Bunny Loyalty Club, the customer confirms that:

  • The information provided is accurate.
  • They have read these Terms & Conditions.
  • They agree to follow the loyalty program rules.
  • They will not attempt to manipulate or misuse the system.
  • They understand that rewards are subject to eligibility and the applicable reward terms.

30. Contact

For questions regarding loyalty stamps, rewards or account issues, customers may contact:

The Bunny Luxury Hair & Beauty Studio

Please contact the salon team directly for assistance with loyalty-account verification or reward redemption.

The Bunny Luxury Hair & Beauty Studio reserves the right to interpret and administer this loyalty program reasonably and consistently with these Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

Nothing in these Terms & Conditions is intended to exclude or restrict any consumer right that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.
